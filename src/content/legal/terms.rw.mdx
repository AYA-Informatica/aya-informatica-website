## 1. Kwemera amabwiriza

Mu kwinjira no gukoresha urubuga ayainformatica.tech («Urubuga»), wemera kugengwa n'aya mabwiriza yo gukoresha. Niba utemera aya mabwiriza, ntugakoreshe Urubuga.

AYA Informatica RW ifite uburenganzira bwo kuvugurura aya mabwiriza igihe icyo ari cyo cyose. Gukomeza gukoresha Urubuga nyuma y'impinduka bisobanura ko wemeye amabwiriza yavuguruwe.

## 2. Uko byemewe gukoreshwa

Ushobora gukoresha uru Rubuga gusa mu ntego zemewe n'amategeko. Wemera kudakora ibi bikurikira:

- Gukoresha Urubuga mu buryo bunyuranyije n'amategeko y'igihugu, y'akarere, cyangwa mpuzamahanga
- Kohereza ubutumwa bw'ubucuruzi butasabwe (spam) unyuze mu ifishi yo kutwandikira
- Kugerageza kwinjira mu gice icyo ari cyo cyose cy'Urubuga utabifitiye uburenganzira
- Kwinjiza virusi, porogaramu mbi, cyangwa ikindi kode yangiza
- Gukurura cyangwa gukusanya amakuru y'Urubuga mu buryo bwikora utabiherewe uruhushya rwanditse
- Kwiyitirira irindi zina cyangwa irindi shyirahamwe igihe udutumaho

## 3. Umutungo bwite mu bwenge

Ibirimo byose kuri uru Rubuga — harimo inyandiko, amashusho, ibirango, udushushondanga, amazina y'ibicuruzwa (RAY, Humura), n'isura rusange — ni umutungo bwite wa AYA Informatica RW kandi birinzwe n'amategeko areba uburenganzira bw'umuhanzi, ibirango, n'umutungo bwite mu bwenge.

Ntushobora gusubiramo, gukwirakwiza, guhindura, gukora ibindi bishingiye kuri, cyangwa gucuruza ibirimo byose by'uru Rubuga utabiherewe uruhushya rwanditse.

## 4. Kwanga ubwishingizi

Urubuga rutangwa «uko ruri» kandi «uko ruboneka» nta bwishingizi ubwo ari bwo bwose, buba bwerekanwe cyangwa bwumvikana, harimo ariko bitarangiriye ku bwishingizi bwumvikana bw'ubucuruzi, bwo kuba bukwiye ku ntego runaka, cyangwa bwo kutarenga ku burenganzira.

AYA Informatica RW ntiyemeza ko Urubuga ruzahora rukora nta guhagarara, nta makosa, cyangwa nta virusi n'ibindi bice byangiza.

## 5. Ugarukira ku nshingano

Mu rugero rwose rwemewe n'amategeko, AYA Informatica RW ntizaryozwa ibyangiritse biturutse ku buryo butaziguye, ibyabaye ku bw'impanuka, ibyihariye, ibikurikira, cyangwa ibihano biturutse ku gukoresha kwawe Urubuga cyangwa bifitanye isano na ko, n'ubwo twaba twaramenyeshejwe ko ibyo byangiritse bishoboka.

## 6. Amahuza y'abandi

Urubuga rushobora kuba rufite amahuza ajya ku mbuga z'abandi. Ayo mahuza atangwa gusa kugira ngo bikworohere. AYA Informatica RW nta bubasha ifite ku birimo cyangwa ku bikorwa by'izo mbuga kandi ntiyemera inshingano izo ari zo zose kuri zo. Guhuza n'urubuga rw'undi ntibisobanura ko turwemera.

## 7. Amategeko agenga

Aya mabwiriza agengwa kandi asobanurwa hakurikijwe amategeko ya Repubulika y'u Rwanda. Impaka zose ziturutse kuri aya mabwiriza cyangwa zifitanye isano na yo zizaburanishwa gusa n'inkiko z'u Rwanda.
