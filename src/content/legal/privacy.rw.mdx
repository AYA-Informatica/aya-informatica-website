## 1. Incamake

AYA Informatica RW («twe», «twebwe» cyangwa «yacu») ikoresha urubuga ayainformatica.tech («Urubuga»). Iyi politiki y'ibanga isobanura amakuru bwite dukusanya, uko tuyakoresha, n'amahitamo ufite.

Mu gukoresha Urubuga rwacu, wemera ibikorwa bisobanuwe hano. Niba utabyemera, ntugakoreshe Urubuga.

Dukusanya gusa amakuru make akenewe. Ntidukora kwamamaza, nta pigiseli z'abandi zidukurikirana, kandi nta isesengura ry'imyitwarire dukora.

## 2. Amakuru dukusanya

### 2.1 Amakuru utanga wowe ubwawe

Iyo wuzuza ifishi yacu yo kutwandikira:

- **Amazina yombi** — kugira ngo tukwandikire neza
- **Aderesi ya imeli** — kugira ngo dusubize ubutumwa bwawe
- **Nimero ya telefoni** — si ngombwa, gusa niba uyitanze
- **Insanganyamatsiko n'ubutumwa** — ibikubiye mu kibazo cyawe

### 2.2 Amakuru akusanywa mu buryo bwikora

Uwakiriye urubuga rwacu (Vercel) ashobora gukusanya mu buryo bwikora amakuru asanzwe ya seriveri: aderesi IP (ihishwa nyuma y'amasaha 24), ubwoko bwa mushakisha, amapaji wasuye, n'aderesi wavuyeho. Ibi bikoreshwa gusa mu mutekano no mu bikorwa remezo. Ntidukora imyirondoro tuyashingiyeho.

### 2.3 Cookies

Uru Rubuga ntirushyiraho cookies zacu, kandi ntirukoresha isesengura rishingiye kuri cookies. Uwakiriye urubuga ashobora gushyiraho cookie ya tekiniki y'igihe gito ikenewe cyane kugira ngo urubuga rukore. Nta cookies zo kwamamaza cyangwa zigukurikirana zikoreshwa.

## 3. Uko dukoresha amakuru yawe

Dukoresha amakuru utanga gusa mu:

- Gusubiza ikibazo cyawe cyangwa icyifuzo cyo gufatanya
- Gukora itumanaho ribanziriza amasezerano
- Kubahiriza inshingano zishingiye ku mategeko

Ntituzigera tugurisha, dukodesha, cyangwa dusangiza abandi amakuru yawe bwite mu ntego zo kwamamaza.

## 4. Serivisi z'abandi

Dukoresha aba batunganya amakuru bakurikira. Buri wese agengwa na politiki y'ibanga ye bwite:

- **Vercel** — kwakira urubuga na CDN. Amakuru asangiwe: aderesi IP, inyandiko z'ibyasabwe.
- **Utanga SMTP** — kohereza imeli z'ifishi yo kutwandikira. Amakuru asangiwe: ibyuzuzwa mu ifishi (amazina, imeli, ubutumwa).
- **@fontsource** — imyandikire ibitswe iwacu. Nta makuru asangiwe; imyandikire ipakirwa aha ngaha, nta cyo isaba kuri interineti.
